# RFC-001 — Engineering Workflow

**Document ID:** YH-RFC-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Release:** v0.1.0 Foundation

---

# Summary

This RFC defines the standard engineering workflow adopted by Your Harness.

The workflow establishes the sequence of engineering activities that transform a user's Intent into reviewed and approved engineering artifacts.

It serves as the reference process for all future platform capabilities.

---

# Motivation

Engineering work should follow a predictable, traceable and reviewable process.

Rather than allowing implementation to begin immediately after receiving a request, Your Harness structures engineering work into explicit stages.

This approach improves quality, reduces ambiguity and enables governance throughout the engineering lifecycle.

---

# Goals

The engineering workflow aims to:

- Provide a repeatable engineering process.
- Support Specification-Driven Development.
- Maintain Human in the Middle.
- Enable traceability.
- Produce reviewable engineering artifacts.
- Support incremental delivery.

---

# Workflow

Every engineering activity follows the workflow below.

```text
Intent
    ↓
Work Item
    ↓
Specification
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Approval
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Verification
    ↓
Release
```

Each stage produces one or more engineering artifacts.

---

# Workflow Stages

## Intent

Represents the user's desired outcome.

An Intent describes _what_ should be achieved without prescribing implementation details.

---

## Work Item

Represents a governed engineering unit derived from an Intent.

A Work Item defines the scope of engineering work.

---

## Specification

Describes the expected behavior before implementation begins.

Specifications provide a shared understanding of the solution.

---

## Review

Evaluates the Specification and other engineering artifacts.

The purpose of the review is to verify correctness, consistency and readiness.

---

## Approval

Authorizes progression to implementation.

Approval is required whenever defined by governance.

---

## Implementation

Transforms the approved Specification into executable artifacts.

Implementation should remain consistent with the approved Specification.

---

## Verification

Confirms that the implementation satisfies the Specification.

Verification may include:

- Manual review
- Automated testing
- Validation against acceptance criteria

---

## Release

Publishes reviewed and verified engineering artifacts.

A Release represents a stable engineering increment.

---

# Engineering Principles

The workflow follows these principles:

- Design before implementation.
- Specifications before code.
- Human approval where required.
- Incremental delivery.
- Explicit governance.
- Full traceability.

---

# Out of Scope

This RFC does not define:

- Runtime implementation.
- AI provider integrations.
- Specification syntax.
- Programming language support.

These topics belong to future RFCs.

---

# Consequences

Adopting this workflow provides:

- Predictable engineering practices.
- Consistent project execution.
- Improved reviewability.
- Better documentation quality.
- Stronger governance.
- Clear traceability.
