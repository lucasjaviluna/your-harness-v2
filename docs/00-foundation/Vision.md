# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision of the Your Harness project.

It establishes why the project exists, the engineering problems it intends to solve and the principles that guide its evolution.

The Vision is the highest-level normative document of the project.

---

# Vision Statement

Your Harness exists to improve the way software is engineered.

The project promotes an engineering process where planning, specifications, governance and human judgment are first-class citizens.

Artificial Intelligence is used to accelerate engineering activities while remaining under explicit human supervision whenever required by governance.

---

# Mission

Provide an open, modular and specification-driven engineering platform that enables individuals and teams to design, implement, review and evolve software in a structured, transparent and governed manner.

---

# Long-Term Goals

The long-term goals of Your Harness are:

- Improve software engineering practices.
- Reduce ambiguity during software development.
- Encourage specification-driven development.
- Promote traceable engineering decisions.
- Enable modular and replaceable platform components.
- Support different engineering disciplines through a common workflow.
- Keep humans in control of engineering decisions.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Your Harness is founded on the following principles:

- Design before implementation.
- Specifications before code.
- Governance before automation.
- Human judgment over autonomous execution.
- Simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
- Incremental evolution over disruptive redesign.
- Explicit decisions over implicit assumptions.

These principles should guide every engineering decision within the project.

---

# Scope

Your Harness is designed to support engineering activities such as:

- New features
- Bug fixes
- Refactoring
- Technical debt
- Testing
- Documentation
- Analysis
- Architecture
- Frontend development
- Backend development
- Quality assurance

The platform focuses on engineering processes rather than specific technologies.

---

# Core Values

The project values:

- Transparency
- Traceability
- Maintainability
- Consistency
- Collaboration
- Continuous improvement

These values should be reflected in both the platform and its community.

---

# Open Source Commitment

Your Harness is developed as an open-source project.

The project encourages collaboration, knowledge sharing and community participation while maintaining engineering quality through documented processes and governance.

---

# Closing Statement

Your Harness aims to become a trusted engineering platform where software quality emerges from disciplined engineering practices supported by modular tooling, explicit governance and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.
