# Purpose

The Engineering Charter defines the engineering principles that govern the design, implementation and evolution of Your Harness.

It establishes the minimum engineering standards expected for every artifact produced within the project.

---

# Engineering Principles

Every engineering activity shall be guided by the following principles.

## Design Before Implementation

Implementation begins only after the problem has been understood and an appropriate design has been defined.

---

## Specification Before Code

Every implementation shall originate from an approved specification.

Specifications describe the expected behavior before development begins.

---

## Human in the Middle

Human approval is mandatory whenever required by project governance.

Automation assists engineering activities but does not replace engineering responsibility.

---

## Traceability

Engineering decisions shall be traceable.

Whenever practical, every implementation should be traceable to:

- Intent
- Work Item
- Specification
- Review
- Approval

---

## Incremental Delivery

Engineering work should be delivered in small, reviewable increments.

Large implementations should be divided into manageable Work Items.

---

## Simplicity

Solutions should remain as simple as possible while satisfying the defined requirements.

Unnecessary complexity should be avoided.

---

## Consistency

New artifacts shall follow the terminology, conventions and standards established by the project.

Consistency is preferred over individual preferences.

---

## Documentation

Documentation is part of the implementation.

Changes affecting behavior or architecture should update the corresponding documentation.

---

## Quality

Quality is built throughout the engineering process rather than verified only at the end.

Engineering activities should prioritize:

- Readability
- Maintainability
- Testability
- Reviewability
- Traceability

---

## Modularity

The platform should be organized into well-defined modules with explicit responsibilities.

Components should communicate through stable contracts whenever possible.

---

# Decision Making

Significant engineering decisions should be documented through the project's defined decision process.

Architectural decisions are expected to remain explicit and reviewable.

---

# Scope

This charter applies to:

- Documentation
- Specifications
- Source code
- Architecture
- Tooling
- Governance
- Reviews

---

# Closing Statement

Engineering excellence is achieved through discipline, consistency and continuous improvement.

The Engineering Charter establishes the principles that enable Your Harness to evolve in a sustainable and predictable manner.
