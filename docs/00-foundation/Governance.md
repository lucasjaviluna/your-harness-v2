# Purpose

This document defines how Your Harness evolves through explicit governance.

Governance exists to ensure that engineering decisions remain transparent, reviewable and accountable throughout the lifetime of the project.

---

# Objectives

The governance model aims to:

- Ensure engineering consistency.
- Preserve architectural integrity.
- Enable transparent decision making.
- Support sustainable project evolution.
- Maintain traceability across engineering activities.

---

# Governance Principles

The governance of Your Harness is based on the following principles.

## Explicit Decisions

Important engineering decisions should be documented rather than assumed.

---

## Human Accountability

Engineering responsibility always belongs to people.

Automation supports decision making but never replaces accountability.

---

## Transparency

Engineering decisions should be understandable by contributors and reviewers.

Project artifacts should be openly accessible whenever possible.

---

## Traceability

Engineering work should remain traceable from the original Intent through implementation and release.

---

## Continuous Review

Governance is an ongoing process.

Engineering artifacts may be reviewed whenever improvements or corrections are required.

---

# Roles

## Sponsor

Provides strategic direction and approves major milestones.

---

## Maintainer

Protects the long-term quality and consistency of the project.

---

## Contributor

Proposes, implements and documents engineering work.

---

## Reviewer

Evaluates engineering artifacts and determines whether they satisfy the project's quality expectations.

---

# Approval

Engineering work requiring governance shall not advance without the required approval.

Approval represents authorization to continue to the next stage of the engineering process.

---

# Compliance

All official project artifacts shall comply with:

- Vision
- Engineering Charter
- Governance
- YDL
- YDS
- YGov

---

# Evolution

The governance model may evolve over time.

Changes shall remain documented, reviewable and traceable.

---

# Closing Statement

Governance exists to enable long-term project sustainability through disciplined engineering practices and responsible decision making.
